# Solutions to Griffiths Introduction of Quantum Mechanics 3rd Edition
To learn Quantum Mechanics, it's best to begin with clear guidance. This repository is designed to help those who wish to self-study the subject. By using this resource, along with a few other relevant online materials, you can master the principles of Quantum Mechanics without needing to enroll in formal college courses. The only material needed is "Introduction to Quantum Mechanics(3rd edition)" by David J. Griffiths and Darrell F. Schroeter.

## Prerequisites
1. A background in a Bachelor of Science degree, preferably in a Physics-related major or an equivalent field, is required. At a minimum, you should have knowledge of Theoretical Mechanics.
2. A background in a Bachelor of Science degree, preferably in an Applied Mathematics major or an equivalent field, is required. You should at least be familiar with Advanced Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Probability and Statistics, Ordinary Differential Equations, and Partial Differential Equations. In fact, you can self-study the last two topics using books like "Elementary Differential Equations" by C. Henry Edwards and David E. Penney, and "Partial Differential Equations and Boundary Value Problems with Fourier Series" by Nakhle H. Asmar.
